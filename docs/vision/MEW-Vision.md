# Manufacturing Engineering Workbench Vision

## Purpose

MEW provides a persistent, reproducible execution environment for maintaining, validating, consolidating and completing BPMN 2.0 engineering models.

Its first operational focus is the multisite template-management model for global MES/MOM and SCADA solutions. The same foundation shall later support Global Manufacturing Change Management, requirement assessment and generation of requirement specifications.

## Product goals

MEW shall:

1. read and represent BPMN 2.0 models semantically;
2. detect structural, referential and methodological defects;
3. support controlled correction and consolidation;
4. preserve traceability between models, roles, requirements and documents;
5. generate reproducible engineering outputs;
6. evolve into a reusable manufacturing-engineering workbench.

## Guiding principle

> As much structure as necessary, as little process as possible.

Governance is introduced only where it improves reproducibility, quality or maintainability.
